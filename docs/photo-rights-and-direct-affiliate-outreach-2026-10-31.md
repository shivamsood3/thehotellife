Each image request covers non-exclusive editorial use on the THL website, mobile/PWA, article cards, search and social previews, and related newsletter promotion. We asked for the exact file, rights holder, photographer credit, crop and resizing rules, expiry, restrictions, and any fee. Any photo grant must be independent of a commercial relationship or review outcome. In particular, [Jumeirah's media-hub terms](https://mediahub.jumeirah.com/terms-of-use/?lang=eng) and [Mandarin Oriental's legal notice](https://www.mandarinoriental.com/en/legal-notices) do not allow website imagery to be republished merely by adding credit.

The direct booking questions seek trackable links, attribution window, qualifying booking rules, cancellation handling, reporting, commission rate, and payment terms. Do not promise rankings, favourable coverage or editorial approval in exchange for either images or commission. Confirm that a proposed direct route actually pays before replacing a functioning OTA partner link.
