Each image request covers non-exclusive editorial use on the THL website, mobile/PWA, article cards, search and social previews, and related newsletter promotion. We asked for the exact file, rights holder, photographer credit, crop and resizing rules, expiry, restrictions, and any fee. Any photo grant must be independent of a commercial relationship or review outcome. The remaining official-site credits identify sources but do not establish permission. In particular, [Jumeirah's media-hub terms](https://mediahub.jumeirah.com/terms-of-use/?lang=eng) require express permission for reuse. Follow up if no reply arrives, and remove or replace any image the rights holder declines to licence. Mandarin Oriental supplied the selected review images directly in response to the editorial request; use them only in this review and its related site cards and previews unless broader use is confirmed.

The direct booking questions seek trackable links, attribution window, qualifying booking rules, cancellation handling, reporting, commission rate, and payment terms. Bella's reply did not address referral terms; a follow-up asked for the relevant contact. Do not promise rankings, favourable coverage or editorial approval in exchange for either images or commission. Confirm that a proposed direct route actually pays before replacing a functioning OTA partner link.
