## Proposed commercial structure — not agreed terms

- Open with **10% of eligible net room revenue** for completed, paid, non-refunded stays. An 8–12% negotiation range is a proposed THL starting point, not an asserted industry entitlement or an offer from any hotel.
- Exclude taxes, service charges, cancellations, refunds and extras unless expressly included in writing. Define currency, FX treatment and any withholding in the contract.
- Request a **30-day referral window**, a dedicated landing link, and a referral identifier stored against the actual reservation. Agree attribution precedence, cross-device limitations, promo-code treatment and duplicate-claim handling. A UTM tag or THL code alone does not create an entitlement to payment.
- Hotel remains merchant of record: payment collection, guest service, cancellation and refunds stay with the hotel. THL must not receive card details.
- Monthly reconciliation of pseudonymous reservation ID, checkout date, eligible revenue, booking status and commission; propose payment within 30 days of the approved monthly statement. Define dispute deadlines and clawbacks.
- Hotel supplies an auditable dashboard or signed report. Verify attribution through the hotel's approved test process before marking a route as tracked; do not create fake paid bookings.
- Keep editorial decisions independent, disclose affiliate relationships and label commercial links appropriately. No promise of ranking, positive reviews or editorial veto in exchange for commission or imagery.
